## Employment Support

Employment Support Advisers are available within Talking Therapies. They are **not** connected to the Job Centre, and their support will not affect any benefit claims. They can help with:

- Remaining in work, returning to work, or finding work
- CV support, interview preparation, job applications
- Speaking to employers about reasonable adjustments or phased returns
- One-off benefit advice sessions
- Volunteering and training support

Support is one-to-one, via face-to-face, telephone, or video call. It is entirely voluntary and can end at any time without affecting your therapy. Employment support can continue even after therapy ends.

**To access:** Ask your practitioner for a referral at any point during treatment.
